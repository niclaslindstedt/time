type: Changed
title: Uptown's minute track, read by its hands
---

The Uptown dial's minute track now carries the same length of tick on the face as on the ring, in the ring's own colour and with two finer marks between each minute, and the hands are read against it: the minute hand stops on the ticks' tips and the second hand crosses the ring's, a dark hair on the blue. Its second hand is balanced by the tapering blade of a dress watch rather than a stub. [Learn more](feature:themes)
