title: The report, read at a glance
---

The Report's four figures are now drawn rather than printed, the way the Log's
are: one ring is the range's share of its target, filling clockwise and going
round again in orange past it, and the other is the balance — red when you are
short, green when you are ahead — each with its pair of figures underneath.
Under them, a week is one column a day instead of two: the target is the track
and the hours worked fill it from the floor up, carrying on past the top in
orange when the day ran long, so a shortfall is the gap left in the track
rather than two bars to compare. [Learn more](feature:report)
