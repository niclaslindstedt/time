type: Changed
title: **The markers and hands are metal**
---

Every applied part of the dial — the hour markers and the hands — is polished steel now rather than ink, drawn from the light that is on it: a block, a wedge and a triangle as two flat faces put together at an angle, tipping up to the ridge where they meet, and a diver's dot as a turned dome with the light in a band across it. Which face of a marker is the bright one depends on where it stands, so the dial is lit the way a real one is instead of evenly.
