title: The month as a calendar of boxes
---

A month's hours are no longer a bar per day under a smear of date numbers: the month is drawn a row to the week and a box to the day, as wide as the hours that day worked and coloured from red through green to blue by how the day went against its target, with the rows stacking to the month's total — and pointing at a box names that day, at the rest of its row that week.
