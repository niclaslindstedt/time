title: The phone app keeps to the device and iCloud for now
doc: native-app
---

The iPhone and Android app does not offer your own storage server for now: your hours stay on the phone, or in your own iCloud on an iPhone, and a phone that was paired with a server opens on its own copy with every day in it, leaving the copy on the server where it was — the website and the desktop app keep the server.
