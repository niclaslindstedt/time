title: A break runs until you end it
doc: today
---

A break you forget to end is no longer cut off at its usual length and the rest counted as work — a coffee ended after twenty-five minutes is twenty-five minutes of coffee. The usual length is now what the clock expects: drawn ahead of the hands, printed on the rim and on the line under the dial, and a break still going can be given its end from the day's stretches.
