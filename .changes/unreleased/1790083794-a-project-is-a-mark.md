---
type: Changed
title: A project is a mark, not a menu
---

A project now wears a glyph and a colour of its own, picked in the project form beside its name. With two or more projects that mark is the switcher: it sits in the top left corner, and over the watch it floats there rather than keeping a whole bar alive for one word. Pressing it lists every project, each in its own mark and colour, to switch with a tap. [Learn more](feature:projects)
