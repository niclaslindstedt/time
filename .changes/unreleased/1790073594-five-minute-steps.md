---
type: Changed
title: Step a break's minutes five at a time
---

The number field's own up/down arrows are gone from a break's **Minutes**, replaced by **−5m** and **+5m** buttons as tall as everything else in the form — five minutes being the amount a lunch is actually nudged by, and the arrows having been a four-pixel mouse target stepping by one. Type a number straight in for the odd break that is not a multiple of five.
