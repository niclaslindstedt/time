title: **Reflections that follow the device**
---

Switch on Reflections under Settings → The clock and the light on the dial's metal follows the device: tilt the phone and it slides across the markers and the hands, each one answering for itself, the way a watch catches the light when you turn your wrist. The motion readings are used for the next frame and nothing else — never stored, never sent.
