title: **Uptown, and the day on a minute ring**
---

A ninth dial, the sixties dress watch: a silver face, long applied blocks, and the day drawn on a deep blue minute ring printed with the minutes — a tick a minute on the ring's inner edge, the numerals above them, and a finer track on the face under it — work, breaks and kinds of work fill the ring, and the minutes stay printed over them. Under Custom the ring is a choice of its own, and the print's light grotesque is a ninth numerals face.
