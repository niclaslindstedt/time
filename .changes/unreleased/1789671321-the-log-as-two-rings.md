type: Changed
title: The log, read at a glance
---

The Log's four figures are now drawn rather than printed: the day's stretches
sit on a twelve-hour dial with the started and stopped hands — so a day worked
in two shows the gap between them — and worked against breaks is a ring split
in green and orange with the day's total in the middle. Each row's times are
pills either side of a dash, the button that adds a span is a **+** in its
section's corner, and deleting the whole day has moved to a **…** menu beside
the date. [Learn more](feature:log)
