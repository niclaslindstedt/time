The mark picker now offers a break type the day's pauses and a kind of work the work marks, with the neutral marks open to both — so a break cannot end up carrying a pair of angle brackets, and a kind of work cannot end up carrying a cup. A mark stored the other way round is dropped when the document is read, leaving that kind with the cup or the label its sort starts out with. [Learn more](feature:projects)
