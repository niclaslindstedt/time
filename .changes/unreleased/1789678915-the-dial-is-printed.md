title: **The name and the cog are on the dial**
---

The watch is printed the way a watch is: the app's mark and name under twelve, the movement's word under them, and the Settings cog in a window above six where a date would be. Over the watch the top bar carries neither, and on a phone, with nothing else to show, there is no bar at all — the watch is the top of the screen.
