title: A toilet mark, and marks for the kinds the app suggests
---

The break marks now include a toilet drawn as one, with the drop it used to be kept alongside it. And the kinds a new project opens with — Lunch, Coffee, Toilet, Meetings, Planning, Retro and Admin — are given their marks wherever they appear: a project made before there were marks now shows a fork on its Lunch and a cup on its Coffee rather than the same cup on every break.
