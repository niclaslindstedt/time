type: Changed
title: A phone on its side is a desk clock
---

Turning the phone sideways used to crop the watch against the top of the screen and put every break below the fold; now the day's controls stand either side of the dial the way they do on a desk, and the dial takes the height there is — so a phone propped up at work is a clock with every break and kind of work one press away.
