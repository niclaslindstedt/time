title: Uptown's track, plots and second hand, read off the dial
---

The Uptown watch face is closer to the dial it is drawn after: the thirds of a minute an hour's block stands over are no longer printed, so an hour shows one mark beside it and twelve shows none; the lume at each hour is a small rounded block on the minutes' own track rather than a large round dot; and the second hand's tail leaves the hub as a hair and swells towards its end, where a counterweight's weight belongs.
