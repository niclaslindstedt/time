title: A mark for every kind, and a colour for a kind of work

Break types and kinds of work now carry a mark of their own — fifty-two to choose from, weighted towards work done at a computer — and a kind of work can be given one of eight colours, which it then wears on the clock, on its chip and in the report; both are picked in the project form, or when a kind is invented from the Today screen's Custom pill.
