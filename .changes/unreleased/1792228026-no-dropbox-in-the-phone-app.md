type: Removed
title: No Dropbox in the phone app
doc: native-app
---

The iPhone and Android app no longer offers Dropbox: it keeps your hours on the phone, or in your own iCloud on an iPhone, and a phone that had Dropbox connected opens on its own copy with every day in it, leaving the file in Dropbox where it was — the website and the desktop app keep Dropbox.
