type: Added
title: Export the range as a PDF specification
---

The **…** beside the week or the month on the Report screen now exports those hours as a specification to send with an invoice: six built-in styles or one of your own, the hours as a period total, a row per day or every stretch of every day, decimal hours that add up, optional rounding up to the next 5 to 60 minutes a day, and either a downloaded PDF or a print. It is written on the device — nothing about the range is sent anywhere. [Learn more](feature:report)
