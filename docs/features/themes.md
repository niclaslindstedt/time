# Themes

Two, plus "follow the device": **Light**, **Dark**, **Device**, under
**Settings → Appearance**. The default follows the device.

The framework the app is built on ships a dozen palettes and a full
appearance picker; this app deliberately exposes none of them. A tool that
gets a few taps a day earns nothing from a theme gallery, and every extra
palette is another surface to keep legible.

## The clock

**Settings → The clock** has the one other thing about the look that is
worth choosing, and it is a _shape_, not a palette:

- **Look.** **Classic** is the wall clock — all twelve numerals and sixty
  minute ticks. **Minimal** drops the numerals and the second hand and thins
  the rings, leaving the day and enough of a dial to read a hand position
  off. **Bold** is the one to read across the room: heavy hands, big
  numerals, thick rings.
- **Size.** Small, medium or large. The face is square, so this is its
  height as much as its width — a large dial on Today pushes the break
  buttons down, a small one keeps the whole screen in one view.

Neither touches colour. Whichever dial is on, time at work is the accent,
breaks are the flag colour, and a kind of work is the hue the chips and the
report already gave it — so the two themes above stay the only choice about
what the app is coloured.
